Step 2

Response comparison

Prewritten example content

A

Analytical

Structured lens

Start by naming the decision, the constraints, and the criteria for success. Compare each option against effort, expected impact, risk, and reversibility before choosing a first experiment.

Emphasis: assumptions and decision criteria.
C

Creative

Idea lens

Reframe the choice as a portfolio. Reserve most effort for the strongest current option, while keeping a small, time-boxed experiment for an unusual idea that could reveal a better direction.

Emphasis: alternatives and reframing.
P

Practical

Execution lens

Choose the smallest test that can answer the most important question. Set a short review date, define what evidence would change the decision, and avoid committing the entire team before learning.

Emphasis: sequencing and low-risk action.
S

Skeptical

Risk lens

Check whether the team is assuming that visible demand represents the full market. Identify the riskiest belief, look for disconfirming evidence, and define a stop condition before investing more time.

Emphasis: evidence gaps and downside risk.

Step 3

Synthesis

Transparent summary

Common ground

Begin with a limited experiment rather than a full commitment. Make the decision criteria visible before comparing options.

Different emphasis

The analytical lens prioritizes a decision framework. The creative lens expands options. The practical lens narrows the next action.

Uncertainty to keep visible

The example does not include market data, team capacity, or user research. A real decision would need relevant evidence.
